## 1. Introduction
This governance framework (GF) provides a set of guidelines and principles for the governance of the Open Commercial Media Ecosystem (OCME) trust community. This GF outlines the roles and responsibilities of the governing authority and governed party, as well as the processes and procedures for managing the OCME ecosystem in a secure, transparent, and accountable manner.
This GF has been structured following the Trust Continuum methodology. It is partially based on the ToIP Foundation's Governance Metamodel Specification. The ToIP Foundation and the ToIP stack play a critical role in advancing the field of decentralized identity, and this GF is designed to align with the foundation's principles and objectives.

## 6. Purpose
The purpose of this Governance Framework is to establish the trust infrastructure that enables the OCME to offer creator governed content. A model where creativity, distribution, and economic value are controlled by those who create it, not corporate gatekeepers. This GF provides the policies, trust mechanisms, and transparent value flows necessary to make that vision a reality across all content verticals within the OCME ecosystem.

## 7. Scope
This Primary Document defines universal governance applicable to all OCME participants regardless of content domain. Colony-specific governance is defined in Colony Governance Extensions (Section 12.2), which inherit and extend this framework for their respective domains.

### 7.1 Key Roles
The following roles are subject to the rules and requirements of this Governance Framework:

- **Creators**: Individuals or groups who create content and execute split sheets for inclusion in the OCME ecosystem.
- **Curators**: Individuals or groups who assemble content into channels and build subscriber audiences.
- **OCME Nonprofit**: The organization responsible for ecosystem governance, treasury management, and operational oversight.
- **Technology Providers**: Entities operating infrastructure components (Trust Registry, Content upload and distribution, etc) on behalf of the ecosystem.

### 7.2 Out of Scope
- **Customers** (subscribers, advertisers, viewers): Engage with the ecosystem through commercial transactions only; not subject to governance obligations under this framework.
- Use of OCME-issued credentials outside the OCME ecosystem.
- Governance of OCME nonprofit operational procedures.

## 8. Objectives
This Governance Framework is designed to achieve the following objectives:

**Creator Economic Agency**

- Ensure transparent, verifiable revenue distribution to all rights holders
- Eliminate "black box" unclaimed funds through precise attribution and automated settlement
- Protect creator ownership through decentralized identity and verifiable split sheets

**Trust and Verification**

- Maintain a Trust Registry that enables any party to verify content provenance and rights holder information
- Provide sufficient technical specifications for parties to procure or build verification tools compatible with the OCME ecosystem
- Establish clear criteria for colony recognition and approval

**Digital Attribution**

- Improve accuracy and persistence of digital attribution across distribution channels
- Ensure Content DIDs provide authoritative, machine-readable rights holder data
- Enable attribution to survive content aggregation, remixing, and redistribution

**Ecosystem Growth and Coherence**

- Drive OCME adoption across content domains through the colony model
- Enable colony autonomy while maintaining interoperability and shared trust infrastructure
- Ensure governed parties are accountable to ecosystem standards

**Creator Governance**

- Enable meaningful creator participation in governance decisions
- Ensure the framework evolves based on creator needs and input

## 9. Principles
The following principles guide all policies, decisions, and operations within the OCME ecosystem. All colonies inherit these principles.

1. **Decentralization & Member Empowerment** - OCME will embrace and champion Creator Governed Content
2. **Transparency & Open Governance** - All decisions visible to stakeholders
3. **Built on Proven, Open Foundations** - CGC only works if creators aren't locked in
4. **Creator Data Autonomy** - Creator autonomy extends to their data
5. **Creator Rights & Fair Compensation** - Value flows to those who create it
6. **Colony Autonomy with Universal Alignment** - Flexibility within guardrails
7. **Long-term Community** - Minimum viable value extraction approach
8. **Inclusion, Equity & Accessibility** - Open to all creators
9. **Minimal Control** - Impose only the governance necessary

## 10. Revisions
- At a minimum, this Governance Framework MUST be reviewed annually.
- Interim revisions MAY be triggered by changes in laws or regulations, proposals from ecosystem participants, or changes to ecosystem services, technical architecture, or Colony structure.
- Revisions to this Primary Document are subject to the following policies:
	- All revisions MUST be identified with a revision number that is a sequential integer.
   - Revisions SHALL be subject to community review by members of the OCME ecosystem for a period of at least 14 days.
- Revisions to Controlled Documents are subject to the following policies:
   - All revisions MUST be identified with a revision number that is a sequential integer.
   - Controlled Documents SHALL be subject to community review by members of the OCME ecosystem for a period of at least 14 days.
- Revisions to Colony Governance Extensions MUST be approved by the respective Colony IAC and are subject to OCME IAC compliance review as specified in Section 11.
- Revisions to this Primary Document MUST be approved by the OCME IAC and the OCME Board. Revisions to Controlled Documents MUST be approved by the OCME IAC.

## 11. Extensions
This Governance Framework is extended through Colony Governance Extensions. Each Colony Extension inherits this Primary Document and defines governance specific to its content vertical.

- A Colony Extension MUST explicitly reference and maintain compliance with this Primary Document.
- A Colony Extension MUST establish a Colony IAC as its governing body for colony-specific decisions.

The following Colony Governance Extensions are recognized by OCME:

**Video Media Colony Governance Extension**
Defines governance for the video media content vertical including music videos, episodic shows, short and feature films. Governed by the Video Media Colony IAC.
