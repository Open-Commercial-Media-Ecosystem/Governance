This glossary defines terms used throughout the OCME Governance Framework. Terms are organized into two sections:

1. **ToIP Core Terms** — Standard terms from the Trust over IP Foundation used in this governance framework. Definitions are provided inline with links to authoritative sources.
2. **OCME Terms** — Terms specific to the OCME Governance Framework.

---

## ToIP Core Terms

The following terms from the [ToIP Core Glossary](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary) and [ToIP Governance Glossary](https://trustoverip.github.io/gswg/glossary) are used in this document:

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Administering Authority | The [party](https://trustoverip.github.io/essiflab/glossary#party) tasked with operating the management of a particular [governance framework](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#governance-framework). The administering authority may or may not be the [governing authority](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#governing-authority). |
| Decentralized Identifier (DID) | A globally unique identifier that does not require a centralized registration authority. DIDs enable verifiable, decentralized digital identity. See [DID](https://trustoverip.github.io/essiflab/glossary#decentralized-identifier) in the eSSIF Glossary. |
| DID Document | A document containing information associated with a [DID](https://trustoverip.github.io/essiflab/glossary#decentralized-identifier), including mechanisms such as public keys and service endpoints that a DID subject can use to authenticate and prove their association with the DID. |
| Ecosystem | A set of at least two autonomous [parties](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#party) whose individual work complements that of other members, and is of benefit to the set as a whole. See also **OCME Ecosystem** in OCME Terms. |
| Governed Party | A [party](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#party) whose [actors](https://trustoverip.github.io/essiflab/glossary#actor) perform in a role defined by a [governance framework](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#governance-framework). |
| Governing Authority | The [party](https://trustoverip.github.io/essiflab/glossary#party) responsible for governing a particular [governance framework](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#governance-framework). The governing authority may or may not be the [administering authority](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#administering-authority). |
| Governance Framework | A set of business, legal, and technical definitions, policies, specifications, and contracts by which the members of a [trust community](https://trustoverip.github.io/gswg/glossary#trust-community) agree to be governed in order to achieve their desired objectives. |
| Trust Registry | A repository which contains a machine-readable listing of approved [governed parties](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#governed-party) deemed compliant by a [governing authority](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#governing-authority) over its attributable criteria of its [governance framework](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#governance-framework). |

## OCME Terms

The following terms are specific to the OCME Governance Framework.

| Term                            | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Channel                         | The distribution mechanism through which curators broadcast content to audiences. Each curator operates one or more channels — whether streaming services, download platforms, or other delivery systems — to feature content from the Content Library in their curated shows. Channels facilitate access and amplification by connecting creators to audiences at scale, removing friction between supply and demand. They provide the means by which monetization is solicited and realized whether through sponsorship, contribution, advertisement, subscription, or other means. Revenue flows through channels based on how and where content is consumed, with all channels operating under the ecosystem's revenue split rules as defined in CD-005. |
| Colony                          | A content vertical within the OCME Ecosystem organized around a specific domain (e.g., video media, music, games). Each colony integrates creators, curators, channels, and infrastructure into a transparent, value-aligned system — ensuring that originality, contribution, and collaboration are recognized, attributed, and rewarded. Colonies inherit the Primary Document and extend it with domain-specific governance through a Colony Governance Extension.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Colony Governance Extension     | A governed document that inherits the Primary Document and defines governance specific to a content vertical. Each Colony Governance Extension MUST explicitly reference and maintain compliance with the Primary Document and MUST establish a Colony IAC as its governing body.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Colony IAC                      | The Industry Advisory Council for a specific colony. The Colony IAC serves as the governing body for colony-specific decisions and must maintain compliance with the OCME Primary Document. Colony IAC decisions are subject to OCME IAC compliance review.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Community                       | A networked collective of individuals who exchange feedback and support, share methods and audiences, share tools and techniques, may collaborate, share infrastructure, and build cooperative visibility. Communities give identity, mentorship, and recognition. Creators gain peer validation and social capital through affiliation. The collective's output amplifies discoverability for individuals. When one member succeeds, the network benefits. A community can also include those who follow or engage with a Creator such as viewers, players, or followers.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Content                         | Any original creative work uploaded by a creator to the OCME Ecosystem — including music videos, films, audio tracks, games, writing, and other creative works. Each piece of content is associated with a split sheet defining its rights holders and may be assigned a DID for persistent, machine-readable attribution across aggregation, remixing, and redistribution.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Content Library                 | The comprehensive repository of all content uploaded by creators to the OCME Ecosystem, serving as the source material from which curators draw to build their shows and programming. This library encompasses diverse media types including music videos, films, audio tracks, games, and other creative works. While creators maintain ownership and control of their work, the Content Library makes their content available for curators to feature across channels, allowing individual pieces to be played in multiple shows and contexts while generating revenue for creators each time their work is broadcast or accessed.                                                                                                 |
| Core DID Types                  | The set of Decentralized Identifier types that are universal to the OCME Ecosystem. All colonies MUST support Core DID Types. Core DID Types are defined and managed in CD-002 Technical Artifacts. New DID types are added through the OCME IAC amendment process.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Controlled Document             | A document maintained as part of the OCME Governance Framework and managed by the OCME IAC. Controlled Documents are identified with the format CD-### and are subject to community review for a period of at least 14 days before revision. Colony-specific controlled documents use the format CD-[ColonyTag]-### and are managed by the respective Colony IAC.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Creator                         | An individual or group that produces original work — music videos, music, films, games, writing, and other creative content. Creators are recognized and compensated primarily for original work and intellectual property. They are the source of unique expression, innovation, and content that drives engagement within the OCME Ecosystem. Creators execute split sheets for inclusion of their content in the ecosystem.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Creator Governed Content (CGC)  | A model where creativity, distribution, and economic value are controlled by those who create it, not corporate gatekeepers. CGC is the foundational philosophy of the OCME Ecosystem.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Curator                         | An individual or organization that adds value through discernment, context, and discovery. Curators are not generating original content but are filtering, organizing, interpreting, and presenting creator work in ways that surface relevance and quality — understanding the needs of the audience with the intention of growing distribution and monetization.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Curator Applicant               | A [party](https://trustoverip.github.io/essiflab/glossary#party) who has submitted an application to join the OCME Ecosystem as a Curator. The application process is a governed process administered by the IAC.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Industry Advisory Council (IAC) | A group of OCME Ecosystem members who advise, administer, and maintain the OCME Governance Framework. The IAC manages many of the day-to-day operations of the ecosystem. See CD-006 for operating details.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Media Registry                  | The ecosystem-wide registry of all media files associated with content in the OCME Ecosystem. The Media Registry is governed by the OCME IAC and serves as the authoritative record of media assets across all colonies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| OCME Ecosystem                  | The totality of participants, infrastructure, governance, colonies, and trust mechanisms operating under the OCME Governance Framework. When "Ecosystem" is capitalized in OCME documents, it refers specifically to the OCME Ecosystem.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Primary Document                | The top-level document of the OCME Governance Framework that defines universal governance applicable to all OCME participants regardless of content domain. Colony Governance Extensions inherit and extend this document.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Show                            | A curated programming block organized and broadcast by curators on their channels, representing a distinct editorial vision with its own theme, format, and schedule. Shows serve as the primary way curators organize creator content into discoverable, audience-focused experiences. A single curator may run multiple shows across their channel(s) to target different audiences or content styles.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Split Sheet                     | A document listing all creators who contributed to a piece of content and their respective percentage revenue share for revenue generated from commercial interactions with that content. Split sheets are executed by creators upon inclusion of content in the OCME Ecosystem and serve as the basis for automated revenue distribution.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Technology Provider             | An entity operating infrastructure components (Trust Registry, content upload and distribution, etc.) on behalf of the OCME Ecosystem. Technology Providers are [governed parties](https://trustoverip.github.io/toip/glossary#governed-party) subject to the rules and requirements of this Governance Framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
