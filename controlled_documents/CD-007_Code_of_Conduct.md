## Purpose

OCME is building a community led space where creating, collaborating and sharing your art is in your control. An era of creator governed content.

We envision a thriving creator ecosystem where artists control their own content, governance is transparent and community-led, and economic value flows to those who create it.

This Code of Conduct ("Code") defines how we work together to make that vision real. It protects our community, our members' rights, and the environment where creative collaboration thrives. This Code isn't about restricting creative works, it's about protecting the space so creativity can flourish.

---

## Scope

This Code applies to all OCME members—creators, showrunners, broadcasters, IAC members, Board members, and staff:

1. Within OCME-controlled spaces: OCME platforms, GitHub repositories, official communications channels, and OCME-organized events
2. In any setting where interactions between OCME members could impact OCME's community, reputation, or business relationships

Note that this Code complements, rather than replaces, legal rights and obligations relevant to any particular situation.

## Our Standards

As OCME members, we commit to:

- Treating others with respect, professionalism, and fairness
- Communicating constructively; avoiding demeaning or inflammatory language
- Giving and receiving objective criticism focused on ideas, not individuals
- Being honest about qualifications and disclosing conflicts of interest
- Representing OCME accurately and operating with transparency
- Respecting privacy, confidentiality, and diverse perspectives
- Honoring contractual commitments and respecting business relationships
- Competing fairly and refraining from actions that undermine OCME's mission
- Supporting newcomers and maintaining forward momentum

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Credible threats of physical harm to anyone, especially based on protected characteristics (race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other status)
- Harassment, discrimination, or sustained disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without consent, or threatening to do so
- Plagiarism or falsely claiming others' work as your own
- Impersonating other members, organizations, or OCME itself
- Deliberately spreading false information about OCME, members, or partners
- Public disparagement, defamation, or misrepresenting OCME's operations
- Interfering with business relationships through poaching, deception, or encouraging breach of contracts
- Misusing confidential information or trade secrets
- Retaliation against those who report violations
- Spam, phishing, or other platform abuse

## Leadership Accountability

Members in governance or leadership roles (IAC, Board, Executive Director) hold themselves to higher standards. Leaders commit to being positive stewards of the ecosystem, modeling this Code, addressing violations when observed, maintaining objectivity, recusing themselves from conflicts of interest, and representing OCME professionally. Leaders who violate this Code may face additional consequences, including removal from leadership positions.

IAC members are not expected to publicly defend OCME beyond official organizational statements. If individual members choose to engage publicly in response to controversies, they do so as individuals, not as representatives of OCME, and should maintain professional conduct.

## Reporting Violations

To report violations, contact:

- **Executive Director:** [andy@ocmeco.org](andy@ocmeco.org)
- **OCME Board** (if ED is involved): [alan@nexartis.com](alan@nexartis.com)

Reports should include incident description, individuals involved, documentation, and whether the situation is ongoing. All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality where possible.

## Enforcement

Reports will be acknowledged within 2 business days. The Executive Director (or Board if ED is involved) will investigate and determine appropriate action. OCME aims to resolve violations within 60 days. Decision-makers who are subjects of complaints will be recused.

Consequences may include warnings, temporary suspension, mandatory mediation, removal from leadership, permanent ban, contract termination, or legal action. Consequences are determined by severity, pattern of behavior, impact, and the violator's response.

Individuals may appeal decisions to the Board within 14 days. Board decisions are final.

## No Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports violations or participates in investigations is itself a violation. Honest reporting requires only good faith, not certainty. However, knowingly false reporting intended to harm others is a serious violation.
