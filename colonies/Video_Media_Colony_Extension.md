# Video Media Colony Governance Extension

This document defines governance for the video media content vertical including music videos, episodic shows, short and feature films. Governed by the Video Media Colony IAC.

This Colony Extension inherits and maintains compliance with the [OCME Primary Document](../OCME_Primary_Document.md).

---

## Showrunner Description

### Showrunner Responsibilities and Expectations

Showrunners are responsible for working with creators to produce compelling shows that showcase high-quality content. Their key responsibilities include:

- **Content Curation:**
  - Curate themed programming blocks that align with creator strengths and audience interests.
  - Select content from the Content Library that maintains editorial coherence and quality.
- **Creator Relations:**
  - Foster collaborative relationships with creators, providing feedback and support to enhance the quality of their content.
  - On-board new creators into the OCME Ecosystem.
  - Manage creator communities, ensuring all creators adhere to the governance framework.
- **Show Production:**
  - Produce show compilations to ensure an engaging selection of content.
  - Build compelling streaming content, not just video-on-demand.
- **Quality Assurance:**
  - Monitor content for adherence to standards and guidelines.
  - Ensure that all content meets the technical and creative quality expected by broadcasters and audiences.

### Process to Join OCME as a Showrunner

Any OCME member in good standing MAY apply to become a Showrunner.

1. The applicant submits a Showrunner application.
2. An OCME administrator reviews and approves or denies the application.

### Enforcement

The OCME takes its Showrunners seriously and expects each showrunner to abide by the responsibilities and expectations listed above. If a showrunner is no longer upholding the values expected of them, the IAC may intervene. The IAC should make a best faith effort to work with the showrunner to correct the situation. If the situation can not be corrected the IAC may call a vote to remove the Showrunner. A supermajority of at least 2/3 vote is required to remove a showrunner.

### Additional Information

There is no limit to the number of showrunners the OCME is allowed to have.

## Broadcaster Description

### Broadcaster Responsibilities and Expectations

Broadcasters are responsible for maintaining engaging channels that attract viewers, advertisers, and distribution partners. A Broadcaster inherits Showrunner capabilities. Their key responsibilities include:

- **Channel Management:**
  - Maintain one or more channels that deliver a consistent, high-quality viewing experience.
  - Ensure that all broadcast content adheres to the governance framework, brand guidelines, and labeling requirements.
- **Audience Growth and Distribution:**
  - Promote the channel and its content through various platforms and social media.
  - Build and maintain subscriber audiences across distribution partners.
- **Advertising and Monetization:**
  - Implement advertising strategies to attract new advertisers and Industry Members.
  - Manage sponsorship and revenue relationships in accordance with CD-005.
- **Reporting and Feedback:**
  - Provide regular updates to the Network on the performance and progress of the channel.
  - Offer feedback to showrunners on content performance and suggest improvements for future programming.
- **Value Creation:**
  - As a Broadcaster you are here to add value to the community, not syphon it away. This is done through audience growth, distribution reach, and the monetization opportunities you bring.

The above list of responsibilities is expected to be much closer to a full time job than a couple hours per week, if not multiple full time jobs.

### Process to Join OCME as a Broadcaster

Process is subject to slight variation. This is a governed process and is administered by the IAC.

1. A Broadcaster Applicant fills out an application and submits it.
2. The application form MUST be hosted on the ocmeco.org website such that it is publicly accessible.
3. The IAC reviews and discusses the application. The application must be discussed in an IAC meeting.
4. The IAC holds a vote at the next meeting (where quorum is met) following the discussion. This allows time for any open questions to get answered prior to the vote.
5. The vote will be conducted and a supermajority of at least 2/3 vote is required to accept a new broadcaster.
6. A vote will either accept or reject the applicant.

### Enforcement

The OCME takes its Broadcasters very seriously and expects each broadcaster to abide by the responsibilities and expectations listed above. If a broadcaster is no longer upholding the values expected of them, the IAC may intervene. The IAC should make a best faith effort to work with the broadcaster to correct the situation. If the situation can not be corrected the IAC may call a vote to remove the Broadcaster. A supermajority of at least 2/3 vote is required to remove a broadcaster.

### Additional Information

There is no limit to the number of broadcasters the OCME is allowed to have.
